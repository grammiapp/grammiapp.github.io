Why this exists

Logging food is the whole point of a calorie counter, and scanning a barcode is the fastest way to do it. Some of the biggest apps now put barcode scanning behind a paid plan. Grammi keeps everything you need for daily logging free, for good, and makes money from optional extras instead of ads.

Questions

Is barcode scanning really free?

Yes — free and unlimited, with no daily cap. So are search, manual logging, daily totals, goals, water and weight.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Your diary is stored on your iPhone; the only things that leave it are the barcodes and words you look up.

What if my food isn't in the database?

Photograph the nutrition label. Grammi reads it on your phone, you check the numbers, and the product is remembered for next time.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from your current app and import it in Grammi's settings.
