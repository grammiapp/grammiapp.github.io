🎯 Daily calorie guess

Five foods, one portion each, four answers. Pick the calorie count you think is right. Everyone plays the same foods, and new ones arrive at midnight.

How scoring works

Your pick is scored by how far it is from the real number, as a percentage, so a near miss still earns points. Under 10% off: 100 points · under 25%: 60 · under 50%: 25 · further off: 0. The same rule as the calorie game in the Grammi app.
