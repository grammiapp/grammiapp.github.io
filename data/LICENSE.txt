open-food-facts-paket-*.json: products from the Open Food Facts data export of 14 Nov 2026, selected per country
(branded, complete and consistent nutrition values, most scanned first, at most 60 per brand). Product names were
cleaned for display (letter case, brand name in front); values are per 100 g (100 ml for drinks); obviously
inconsistent values were dropped (sugar above carbohydrates, saturated fat above fat, sodium written in mg).
