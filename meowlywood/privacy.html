Meowlywood — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Meowlywood is a single-player game for iPhone about a city of celebrity cats. It works without an account and, on supported iPhones, without an internet connection.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or transmit personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDK.

How the city writes its posts

The posts, replies and headlines in the game are written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). What you type stays on your device. On iPhones without that model the game uses its own built-in lines.

Optional: in the Me tab you can enter the address of a computer on your own Wi-Fi running the Meowlywood bridge. If you do, the game sends its writing requests (your in-game posts and the city's context) to that computer only, on your local network, and to nowhere else. This is off unless you turn it on.

What stays on your device

Your cat, fans, coins, energy, closet and the feed are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Optional energy packs are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The game receives only the result of a purchase and never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you accept, the game schedules one local reminder a day on your device when your free energy is back. No notification is sent through any server.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
