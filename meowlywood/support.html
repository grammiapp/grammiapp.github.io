Meowlywood — Support

Meowlywood is the city where cats are famous: post, do jobs, dress your cat, and watch the city react.

The city is not talking back

The posts are written by Apple's on-device model, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). On other iPhones the game uses its built-in lines.

Energy

Every post, reply and job costs one energy. You get 10 free every morning. Energy packs are optional and sold through the App Store; to restore or question a purchase, use Apple's standard purchase support.

Starting over

Me tab → Start over. This deletes your cat, fans, coins and closet on this device.
