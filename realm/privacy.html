Realm – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Realm is an offline trivia-conquest game. It does not collect, store or share personal data.

What the app stores

Your progress (campaign state, settings, coins, cosmetics, daily and weekly scores) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

Realm makes no network requests. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information from a purchase. Purchases are consumable and tied to your device's save; restoring them follows Apple's standard process.

Children

The app contains no user-generated content, no chat and no links to social networks. It is suitable for all ages.
