# Grammi: calorie counter for iPhone

Grammi is a free calorie counter app for iPhone, with an Apple Watch app, available in 13 languages.
**No ads. No account.** The food diary is stored on the iPhone. It also works on **iPhone Duo**: it fills the outer display and the
7.6-inch inner display, and adapts when you fold or unfold the phone.

## Free, for good
- Unlimited barcode scanning, backed by the Open Food Facts database of millions of products
- Not in the database? Photograph the nutrition label. Grammi reads the numbers, you check them, and the product is remembered for next time
- Basic foods — eggs, fruit, rice, meat — with real portion sizes, in your language, even offline
- Search, add food by hand, copy yesterday's meals
- Calories, protein, carbs and fat — plus fiber, sugar, saturated fat and sodium
- Daily goals, a weight log with a 7-day average, water and streaks
- Every chart for the last 7 days
- Home Screen widget, Apple Watch app, Siri Shortcuts and Apple Health
- Export your diary as a spreadsheet (CSV) — it's your data
- GLP-1 medication log for semaglutide, tirzepatide or liraglutide: a dose-day reminder, injection-site rotation and a two-tap daily note on appetite and side effects — a log only, never dose advice
- Calorie sense, the daily game: the same five foods for everyone, new every day — guess the calories and share your score
- On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iOS 26): recipes written for your goal, with nutrition calculated from the food database, not guessed

## Grammi Pro (optional)
A monthly or yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

- An adaptive target: your real burn rate, calculated from what you ate and how your weight actually moved — not a formula based on your age
- Weekly challenges, personal records and Streak run: rounds of 5 back to back, counting your perfect rounds and correct answers in a row
- 30- and 90-day charts, plus a chart builder
- Fasting timer and Ramadan mode: suhoor and iftar times calculated on your phone, offline
- Macro planner with separate targets for training and rest days
- Import recipes from a link and log food by voice
- Vitamin and mineral report, suggestions for what fits the rest of your day, and app icons
- Photograph a meal (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iOS 27)
- GLP-1 insights: your appetite on each day after a dose, and your weight with every dose change marked
- Four more looks for the calorie counter: a fuel gauge, a plate of where your calories came from, 200-calorie bites and calories by meal
- Family Sharing: one Yearly or Lifetime purchase covers up to six family members

## Private by design
No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. The diary is stored on the iPhone; speech and photos are processed on the device. The only things
that leave the phone are the barcodes and words you look up, and the recipe links you choose to import.
