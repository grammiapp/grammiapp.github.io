# Grammi: calorie counter for iPhone

Grammi is a calorie counter app for iPhone, with an Apple Watch app, available in 13 languages.

- **Free for good:** barcode scanning with no daily limit and logging every meal (search, manual entry, daily totals), along with goals, weight and water.
- **No ads, no account:** open the app and start logging. The food diary is stored on the iPhone, not in an account.
- **Daily calorie guessing game:** every day everyone gets the same 5 foods and guesses their calories from 4 options. Play it in the app or free in the browser: [grammiapp.com/game](https://grammiapp.com/game/).
- **GLP-1 log:** doses (semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide), the next injection site and a two-tap daily note on appetite and side effects, next to the food diary. A log only: no medical advice, no dose suggestions.
- **Bring your history:** import meals and weigh-ins from another app with a CSV file.
- **iPhone Duo:** fills the outer display and the 7.6-inch inner display, and adapts when you fold or unfold the phone.
- **Grammi Pro** (optional subscription, 7-day free trial): meal photos, an adapted calorie target, 30- and 90-day charts, a fasting timer and more counter looks.
