How many calories are in 100 g of chicken nuggets?

How much chicken nuggets is 100 calories?

How much protein is in a portion of chicken nuggets?

2.5 g in a typical portion (1 piece, 16 g). 21% of its calories come from protein. That is above the 20% the EU requires for a “high protein” claim.
