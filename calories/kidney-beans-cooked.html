Questions

How many calories are in 100 g of kidney beans (cooked)?

127 kcal. 100 g also has 8.7 g of protein, 22.8 g of carbohydrates and 0.5 g of fat.

How much kidney beans (cooked) is 100 calories?

About 79 g. That is 45% of a typical portion (1 cup, 177 g).

How much protein is in a portion of kidney beans (cooked)?

15.3 g in a typical portion (1 cup, 177 g). 27% of its calories come from protein. That is above the 20% the EU requires for a “high protein” claim.
