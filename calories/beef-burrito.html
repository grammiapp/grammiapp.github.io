Questions

How many calories are in 100 g of beef burrito?

225 kcal. 100 g also has 10.8 g of protein, 26.1 g of carbohydrates and 8.3 g of fat.

How much beef burrito is 100 calories?

About 44 g. That is 12% of a typical portion (1 piece, 380 g).

How much protein is in a portion of beef burrito?

41 g in a typical portion (1 piece, 380 g). 19% of its calories come from protein. That meets the EU’s 12% threshold for a “source of protein” claim, but not the 20% for “high protein”.
