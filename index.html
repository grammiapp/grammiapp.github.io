Free calorie app: count calories on your iPhone

Grammi is a free calorie counter app for iPhone. Scan a barcode and the calories, protein, carbs and fat land in your food diary right away. Barcode scanning is free, with no daily limit.

The food diary is free and unlimited too: log every meal by search, barcode or by hand, and see your daily totals at a glance. No ads and no account: your data stays on your iPhone.

Want a quick break? The daily calorie guess asks you to guess the calories in five foods each day, free in your browser.

Questions

Is Grammi free?

Yes. Barcode scanning with no daily limit and logging every meal (search, manual entry, daily totals) are free for good, along with goals, weight and water. Grammi Pro is optional: meal photos, an adapted calorie target, 30- and 90-day charts and a fasting timer, with a 7-day free trial.

Does Grammi have ads?

No. There are no ads in Grammi, on the free plan or on Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start logging. Your food diary is stored on your iPhone, not in an account.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV file from your previous app and import it into Grammi; meals and weigh-ins keep their dates.

Is there a calorie counter app with a game?

Yes: Grammi has a daily calorie guessing game. Every day everyone gets the same 5 foods and guesses their calories from 4 options. Play it in the app or free in your browser: https://grammiapp.com/game/.

Can I track GLP-1 medication like Ozempic® or Mounjaro®?

Yes. Grammi logs your GLP-1 doses (semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide), suggests the next injection site and keeps a two-tap daily note on appetite, nausea and constipation, right next to your food diary, with an optional dose-day reminder. Logging is free, needs no account and stays on your iPhone; the chart of appetite after each dose is part of Grammi Pro. Grammi keeps a log only: it doesn’t give medical advice or suggest doses.

Does Grammi work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Grammi fills the whole screen on iPhone Duo, both the outer display and the 7.6-inch inner display, and its screens resize when you fold or unfold the phone.

Which devices and languages?

Grammi is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app, available in 13 languages.

Stays on your device

Grammi does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
