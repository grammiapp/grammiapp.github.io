Needle Dig — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Needle Dig is a single-player game for iPhone. It is built to work without an account and without an internet connection.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or transmit personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDK.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, farm, settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes this data. The game does not upload it anywhere.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The game receives only the result of a purchase (for example, that a gold pack was bought) and never sees your payment details. Purchase restoration also goes through Apple. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles payments.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
