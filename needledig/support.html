Needle Dig — Support

Needle Dig is a one-finger digging game: find the needle in a haystack of six million strands, sell what you dig up and build a 3D farm.

Common questions

Does the farm produce while the app is closed? No. Fields, animals and workshops only run while you play; nothing piles up while the app is closed.

I bought something but did not receive it. Open the gold shop (the ✦ button) and tap Restore purchases. Permanent items (starter pack, boosts, seasons pack) are restored this way. If a gold pack is missing, write to us with the App Store receipt.

Can I move my progress to a new phone? Not yet. Progress is saved only on the device.

Sound or music is off. Settings (the gear button at the top right) has separate switches for sound effects and music.
